Colour changes & stripes
Stripes are written into the pattern as “Color rnd 4-5: #ffffff” lines, so you can edit them like any other line. The part’s own colour is used for the rest.

Import a pattern
Paste a pattern, or open a .txt, .md or .pdf file, or a photo of a printed pattern (straight on, in good light). You check every line before it opens, and anything loopyduck isn’t sure about is kept as a note. Reading a photo happens on your device; nothing is uploaded.
Design a shape
Pick a shape, a size and a yarn weight and loopyduck writes the rounds. The stitch counts are estimates from typical amigurumi tension, so make a small swatch and adjust under “Your tension”.

Stitch symbol chart
Rounds are drawn as rings from the middle out, flat parts as rows from the bottom. Each ring or row is tinted with its yarn colour. Charts show what the reader understood, so a line it can’t read leaves a gap.

Learn with the duck


Open Library → Duck. The pattern’s notes explain things as they come up. Whenever you like, you can:


  • Finish the body with the keypad and the Fasten off button.
  • Drag the round slider to replay the build, or switch the colour mode.
  • Change a part’s colour or how many to make in Part & join.
  • Open Make for the stuffing amounts and sewing order.
  • Tick Made on a part and turn on Ghost unmade parts.
  • Undo something, then save it as your own project.




Quickstart


  1. Type one round per line. Enter adds the next Rnd N: for you.
  2. Or tap: start with MR, then a number and a stitch. Greyed buttons wouldn’t fit the round. Add round commits it.
  3. Drag the 3D model to rotate. Slider and Follow replay the build. To move a joined part, tap it, tap ✥ Place and drag it where it goes; drag the part itself up or down to lean it.
  4. Open Part & join to attach parts, then set angle and tilt or drag on the model.
  5. Use the Rules tab to switch the stitch-math checks on or off.
  6. Tap ? in the header to label every section.

Pattern syntax


Part: Head          start a new part
Color: #f8fafc      yarn colour (name or hex)
Type: flat          flat | round | pressed
Make: 2             make several (pairs mirror)
Lie: forward        lay the 3D model down, pointing forward
                    (main part only; joined parts turn too)
Mr, 6 sc            round 1: numbering is automatic
Inc x6 (12)         6 increases, (12) checks the count
(2 sc, inc) x6      repeat a group
hdc inc, dc dec      increases and decreases of any
                    stitch (also hdc2tog, invdec)
6 hdc blo           back loop only (flo: front loop)
(fpdc, bpdc) x6     front and back post stitches
1 sc, 1 inc, x6     same, without brackets
(sc, inc) around    repeat to the end of the round
Inc x6              on 12 sts: 6 inc + 6 sc assumed (18)
Repeat rnd 5 x3     copy a round three more times
Stuff firmly        a step marker (also: Insert safety eyes,
                    Sew to the body, Fasten off)
Rnd 4-6: sc around  repeat a round
Define: shell = 5 dc in same st
                    say what a stitch means here so it
                    can be counted (also V-st, cluster)
Join: Ear to Head, rnd 13, angle 45, tilt 12
Join: Ear to Head, rnd 13, 8 sts
                    sew along 8 sts (edge pinched flat);
                    rnd 10-14: down the rounds; both: open
